# Voice guide for @AIAgentHub

## Angle
Practical daily updates on AI agents: new tools, frameworks, and demos, with a clear take on what is actually useful.

## Tone
- Direct, practical, a little opinionated
- No hype words ("game-changer", "revolutionary", "insane")
- Short sentences. One idea per post.

## Every post should have
- A concrete detail (a repo name, a number, a specific result)
- One line on why it matters to someone building agents

## Never post
- Unverified claims or rumors
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree")
- Anything I haven't approved
